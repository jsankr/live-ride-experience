Evidence camera

OBD-II adapter

Use an ELM327 BLE dongle (Vgate iCar Pro BLE, Veepeak BLE, OBDLink CX). Classic-Bluetooth dongles do not work in browsers. Most motorcycles need a brand-specific cable from their diagnostic plug to the 16-pin OBD connector.


        

        

          
Phone mount & sensors

          
Mount the phone rigidly (vibration-damped mount). Orientation does not matter: RideLens learns "up" when you are stationary and "forward" from your first few speed changes.
